# Emmaus privacy policy

**Effective 27 November 2026**

Emmaus is a Bible reader and study app for Android and macOS. It is built so that
reading and note-taking never require an account or a connection.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** There is no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or attribution
SDK in the app, and the developer operates no server that receives your data.

## Where your data lives

- Notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading history, plan progress and settings are
  stored in the app's private storage on your device.
- Export writes a JSON or Markdown file wherever you choose to send it.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

## The optional API.Bible key

NIV, NASB and NKJV are licensed translations and are not included. If you add your
own free API.Bible key:

- The key is stored on your device, encrypted with a key held by the Android
  Keystore (Android) or in a file readable only by your user account (macOS).
- It is sent only to `https://rest.api.bible` when you open one of those
  translations, together with the chapter you asked for.
- Downloaded chapters are cached on your device for 30 days, as the publisher's
  terms require.
- The key is never included in exports, logs or cloud backups.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13.
