## Full description

Emmaus is a study Bible that works without a connection.

**The library is included.** 41 public-domain translations and 1.19 million
verses live on the device: KJV with Strong's numbers and red letters, BSB, WEB,
ASV, YLT, Darby, Douay-Rheims, Geneva 1599, plus Hebrew, Greek, Latin, Arabic,
Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Japanese, Thai,
Persian and more. Nothing is downloaded when you open a chapter.

**The study desk is one tap away.** Tap a word for its Strong's number,
morphology and every other occurrence. Tap a verse number for cross-references,
commentary (Matthew Henry, JFB, Geneva, Scofield, Tyndale), the interlinear line,
a translation comparison and your own notes.

**Search every word.** Quoted phrases, `AND` / `OR`, prefix stems, references,
and Strong's numbers. Hebrew points and Greek accents are optional — type
`אלהים` and it finds `אֱלֹהִים`.

**Read with a plan.** Bible in a year, New Testament in 90 days, Gospels in 60,
Psalms & Proverbs in 31, Paul in 30, Wisdom in 30. Streaks and progress stay on
the device.

**Your notes stay yours.** Highlights, bookmarks, notes and reading history are
stored locally and export as JSON or Markdown. There is no account, no analytics,
no crash uploader and no advertising.

NIV, NASB and NKJV are licensed and not bundled. You can add your own free
API.Bible key in Settings to read them; chapters are cached for 30 days as the
publisher requires.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). macOS build in the same repository.
