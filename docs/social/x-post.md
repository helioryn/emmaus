## Posting notes

- **Attach, don't link.** X does not unfurl an image URL. Upload the files from
  `docs/screenshots/`.
- **Link in the body**, not a reply. If a post has both images and a link, the
  images show and the link stays a link — that is what you want.
- **Alt text on every image.** It is the single cheapest reach and accessibility
  win, and phone screenshots are otherwise unreadable to screen readers.
- **Aspect ratios.** X crops tall images in the timeline. `android-*.jpg` are
  9:20; if they look cropped, upload them two at a time (the pair layout keeps
  more of each) or crop to 4:5 first.
- Suggested tags, sparingly: `#OpenSource` `#Bible` — more than two reads as spam.
- The repo link generates its own GitHub preview card, so no separate OG image is
  needed.
